## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: High-end productivity launch film with sharp tension and seamless resolution.
- Interpretation: Restrained typography, soft layered elevation shadows, smooth Spring easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), zero aggressive cuts, allowing every screen and stat to settle and be fully digested.
- Angle: "Manual Chaos vs. Instant Automation" — Show the painful cost of manual context switching, then unleash the three fastest Fwork workflow automations.
- Hook: Fragmented spreadsheet and chaotic browser tab overload shattered by sleek violet glassmorphism.
- Outro / punchline: "Zero polling. Zero manual chaos. 100% velocity."
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS buzzwords without grounding.
  - Abstract filler graphics.
  - Unrelated visual redesign.
